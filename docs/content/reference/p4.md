# Gröbner bases and membership

These operations compute reduced Gröbner bases over exact coefficient fields, using
Buchberger's algorithm and Gebauer–Möller pair pruning. It selects the least
LCM first, with ties broken by stable pair indices. Over primes below $2^{31}$
under GRLEX and GREVLEX, bases computed without cofactors use F4, which reduces
all pairs of least LCM degree together as one sparse matrix; the result is the
same reduced basis. Over `QQ` under GREVLEX the inputs are homogenized and
the basis is reconstructed from images modulo primes and certified by exact
checks, or computed exactly if reconstruction gives up; the result is again
the same reduced basis. Bases are monic and
sorted by decreasing leading monomial.

LEX, GRLEX, and GREVLEX are accepted; KERNEL is storage order and raises
`DOMAIN`. Generators may arrive in another order and are reordered explicitly.
All generators must belong to one coefficient field. Empty or all-zero
generator lists produce the empty basis.

Cofactor tracking is optional because witnesses can grow much larger than
the basis. Membership enables tracking and returns multipliers in the original
generator order, including zero generators. The checker verifies only the
polynomial identity and does not compute a Gröbner basis.

## Exact complex coefficients

`QQi` represents the field $\mathbb Q(i)$: numbers $a+bi$ with rational
$a,b$. Construct a coefficient as `QQi(ExactComplex(real, imag))`, importing
`ExactComplex` and `Rational` from apn_mojo. Its context is `NoContext()`;
there is no precision parameter. Arithmetic, zero tests, reduction and
membership identities are exact.

The existing `groebner`, `reduce`, `membership`, `MembershipCertificate` and
`check_membership` APIs accept `SparsePoly[QQi]`. LEX, GRLEX and GREVLEX
and optional cofactor tracking have the same semantics as over QQ.

For $I=\langle x^2-iy,xy-1\rangle$ in lexicographic order $x>y$, the reduced
basis is $[x-iy^2,y^3+i]$, and $x^3$ reduces to $i$:

This field includes rational complex numbers, but not coefficients such as
$\sqrt{2}+i$. Rounded `ComplexDomain` and enclosing `ComplexBallDomain` remain
separate numerical domains and cannot instantiate an exact Gröbner basis.
The kernel codec still accepts QQ polynomials only; complex membership
certificates are checked by the library and have no kernel wire encoding.
`QQi` also supports [exact factorization](p3.md). Exact complex algebraic-number
values use `poly.algebraic.Complex` and the inferred `QQbar` field, which also
supports these field-generic Gröbner operations. See
[algebraic coefficients](../tutorial/algebraic-coefficients.md). Numerical complex
roots use certified regions.
