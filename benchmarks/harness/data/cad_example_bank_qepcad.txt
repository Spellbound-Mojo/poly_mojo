#2: Whitney Umbrella
[Whitney Umbrella]
(x,y,z,u,v)
3
(E u)(E v)[[x - u v = 0] /\ [y - v = 0] /\ [z - u^2 = 0]].

#9: Termination of Term Rewrite System

#10: Collins and Johnson
[Collins and Johnson]

#11: Range of Lower Bounds
[Range of Lower Bounds]

#13: Davenport and Heintz
